How to play

Controls

  • ←→ / AD Steer
  • ↑ / W Accelerate
  • ↓ / S Brake
  • Space / Shift Nitro (2× score)
  • P / Esc Pause
  • C Switch camera (cockpit / top)

Scoring

Pass traffic closely for NEAR MISS bonuses. Chain them to build a combo up to x10. Coins go to your wallet for new cars. Every 1.6 km is a new level with a new location and heavier traffic.

Power-ups

  • Shield absorbs one crash
  • Magnet pulls in nearby coins
  • Nitro refills your tank
  • Slow-mo slows down traffic
  • Heart gives an extra life

Hazards

Oil slicks make you skid. Cones kill your speed and combo. Police cars change lanes — watch their blinkers!
